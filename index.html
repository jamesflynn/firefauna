When you arrive

  • Drop off your stuff. Then promptly park across the street. Like over the road.
  • Note the port-a-potties. They will be our toilets for the duration of F.o’26. Please don’t use the house toilets, the plumbing is not festival grade.

Keep in mind

  • It’s camping, and it can get cold. Equip and dress yourself accordingly. Layers. Headlamp. Eyelash curler.
  • Please mitigate MOOP. No feathers that aren’t bolted on. Watch those tent pegs, wrappers etc.
  • After the event you will be asked to pitch in for costs. Est ~$50pp.
    • Tally receipts if you are a designated purchaser (usually meal captains). Any other expenses you want to share, please email for pre-approval.

Do these!

  • Look at the carpool list. Help get friends to Flaming.o
  • Email Anna if you want to bring / support an activity.
  • Text Nina to sign up for Cabaret.
  • Join the All Flaming.o WhatsApp group.

Sheets, contacts and the WhatsApp invite are in the group chat — this page is public, so they’re deliberately not here. Ask an organiser if you’re missing them.
